# Bundled tippytop icons for the Dane default top-site tiles

`browser.newtabpage.activity-stream.default.sites` (browser/app/profile/firefox.js) ships six tiles.
youtube.com, amazon.com and x.com already had upstream tippytop entries. The three entries below are
added to `browser/components/topsites/content/tippytop/top_sites.json`. jar.mn needs no change:
`content/activity-stream/data/content/tippytop/ (./content/tippytop/*)` already packages `images/` and `favicons/`.
With a bundled icon, the new-tab page shows the local image instead of rendering a screenshot of the
tile. Nothing else changes: the screenshot feature, prefs and identifiers are untouched.
TippyTopProvider matches the exact host after stripping `www.`.

Round 5a (2026-11-10): the two coin-site homepage tiles were replaced by
one `https://dane.surf/` tile. The `.doge` tile is unchanged. The previous
coin-site tippytop images were removed from the tree.

## Grokipedia: why a letter tile (round 3, 2026-11-10)
The coordinator ruled that xAI's Grokipedia mark must not be bundled, so the round-2 favicon-derived files
(`images/grokipedia-com@2x.png`, `favicons/grokipedia-com.png`) were removed. Without some tippytop entry, the
new-tab page would capture a screenshot of grokipedia.com for its default tile. The narrowest mechanism that
avoids that, without touching any pref or turning screenshots off globally, is to keep the exact-host tippytop
entry and point it at our own neutral letter tile. The tile only identifies the site by its initial, the same
way a generic letter fallback does.
